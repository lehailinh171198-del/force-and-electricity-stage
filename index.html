Online Class Facilitation Guide

How to run this interactive activity during a Zoom / Teams online class:

1. Planetary Rover (5-8 mins):

Share screen or give link. Ask students to predict what happens to the weight vector arrow when moving from Earth to Jupiter. Ask: "Does the rover get heavier or lighter?" (Answer: Weight increases, mass is unchanged!).

2. Circuit Switch Challenge (8-10 mins):

Have students predict the brightness of Lamp 1 vs Lamp 2 in parallel branches before toggling Switch B. Discuss why series bulbs share voltage while parallel branches receive full voltage.

3. Upthrust Barge Tank (5-8 mins):

Demonstrate the threshold where total weight exceeds maximum fluid upthrust. Connect this directly to the Worksheet 1 Cargo Barge question.

4. Parachute Anomaly Spotting (5-8 mins):

Highlight the 5.9s anomalous result in the data table. Ask students why anomalous results must be discarded when taking averages in science investigations.
